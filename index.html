About Selda

At Selda, we bring the vibrant flavors and warm hospitality of Turkey to San Antonio. Inspired by generations of culinary tradition, our menu celebrates authentic Turkish cuisine prepared with halal meats and premium ingredients.

Learn more

Our Menu

From our vibrant small bites and charcoal-grilled entrees to our traditional, house-made Turkish desserts, every dish is a celebration of Turkey's rich culinary history. Complete your dining experience with our premium selection of Turkish wines, beers, traditional Raki, chilled Ayran, or an authentic, individually brewed Turkish coffee.
